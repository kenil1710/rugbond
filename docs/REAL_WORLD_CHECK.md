Every verdict in [FEASIBILITY.md](FEASIBILITY.md) checked against chain data the rule does not read, from the second RPC of each chain (`tools/real_world.mjs`, [real_world.json](real_world.json)):

- **RUGGED**: the pair's `Burn` event in block R comes from the rug transaction, that transaction was sent by the team (the address that minted the LP), and the pool's WETH reserve collapsed across R.
- **HELD**: the LP holders still held at least the promised LP at the end of the period, and no LP holder sent a transaction that burned LP during it.

Result: **23/23** verdicts agree with the independent signals.

**Held, but the price collapsed** (eth-14, eth-15, eth-18, eth-19): the LP stayed where it was promised, so the rule says HELD, and the WETH left the pool through sells, not through a liquidity pull. RugBond bonds the liquidity promise only; a token whose price goes to zero while its LP stays in place has kept that promise (README, Scope).

Scanner pages are the public record for these tokens: none of them had a public post-mortem; they are anonymous launches that were pulled within hours (the rugs) or kept their LP (the held ones).
