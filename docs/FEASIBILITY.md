# Feasibility

Step 0, before any contract code was trusted: can GenLayer validators read real liquidity pulls (and real liquidity that stayed) from public chain data, and do two independent archive RPCs agree on every field the rule uses?

Every case below was scanned to a verdict with the contract's own reads (contracts/shared/rpc.py, mirrored in tools/lib/rug.mjs), each read made on **both** RPCs of its chain from a Mac, and every check range and two holder snapshots per rug were read again **inside GenVM** by a throwaway probe contract on Studio Dev (`0x0b7C10e05f70f87dfaF72Dc84F3B1F4133E87926`, not part of this repository; the probe concatenates the same shared read code). Generated by `tools/feasibility.mjs` and `tools/feasibility_md.mjs` from [feasibility.json](feasibility.json) and [feasibility_genvm.json](feasibility_genvm.json).

## Result

- **17 real rug pulls** (team removed liquidity: an LP Transfer from the team address to the pair and its Burn) on Ethereum (11) and Base (6), all on Uniswap V2 pools.
- **6 real tokens whose LP stayed in place** for the whole promised period (Ethereum): team-held LP (3), LP locked in the UNCX UniswapV2Locker (1), LP burned to 0x...dEaD (2).
- Rule verdicts from the Mac match the expected outcome for **23/23** cases; both RPCs returned identical facts on every read that produced a verdict.
- GenVM: **39/39** reads (check ranges and holder snapshots) byte-for-byte identical to the Mac's reads of the same ranges, under consensus (leader and validators each reading both RPCs).
- The threshold (8 readable real rugs) is met.

Every log window is its own request; state calls are batched. An HTTP 429 or a disagreement is PENDING, never a verdict.

## What is read, per check (one RPC; the contract does it for both)

1. `eth_chainId` and `eth_getBlockByNumber("finalized")`: the range is the next window after the cursor, cut at the lower finalized head.
2. `eth_getLogs` on the pair, topic0 = Transfer, topic1 = any LP holder, one request per window: every LP Transfer **out of** an LP holder.
3. `balanceOf(holder)` on the pair at the start block (first check) and at each block with such a transfer (up to 6 per check): the summed balance. The first block below min_lp is R.
4. At R: its timestamp, and the token snapshot at R-1: `totalSupply()` and `balanceOf` of every excluded address. Without R: the timestamp of the last block read.
5. First check only: `token0()`, `token1()`, `factory()` of the pair and the factory's `getPair` (or Aerodrome `getPool(a, b, false)`).
Per claim: `balanceOf(holder)` at R-1 and `eth_getCode(holder)`. Responses stay small: logs are filtered by pair and holder topics.
